Exclusions are as important as detections

Do not claim an incorrect amount, counterfeit note, missing electronic receipt, invalid identity check, unauthorized employee, faulty ledger entry, or a failed machine authenticity test unless the necessary information is genuinely visible and the applicable rule supports that conclusion. Do not use customer-counting footage to invoke internal-recount supervision. An apparent phone, bag or open drawer is not automatically a violation. Resolve context before applying a rule.

The National Bank's January 2025 notice explains changes to cash-premises protection under Resolution No. 14, with transition periods. That supports researching premises rules carefully; it does not let the system infer building certification or physical security compliance from a short teller clip. Older repealed cash rules and rules specifically for National Bank branches must not be casually substituted for commercial-bank requirements.

Store rule ID, exact title and URL, clause, applicability, exclusions, revision/effective date, public-source snapshot/hash and the observation-to-rule predicate. The local model selects existing IDs. Legal citations are not free-form model output.

Differentiation that is useful and defensible

Banking video analytics and video-based investigations already exist. March Networks Searchlight for Banking supports synchronized transaction review and audits; its banking case study describes video combined with teller/ATM data. Axis describes analytics for bank branch processes, and BriefCam discusses video review for compliance. Therefore, “AI finds suspicious banking activity” is an established category.

The proposed differentiation is the specific combination: video-only operation without bank-system integration, local deployment, Kazakhstan rules with explicit scope, and a reviewer interface that exposes the proof and its limits. The reviewed product pages do not establish that no competitor offers this combination. Describe it as the entry's focused approach, not a world-first claim or a patentability finding.

Inference and evidence

Cover the complete clip with bounded temporal windows, then refine candidate moments at higher sampling density. Keep uniform sampling so low-motion omissions remain visible. A local verifier checks both the alleged condition and plausible non-violating context. The rule engine accepts only supported predicates. Extract original JPEG evidence by actual frame timestamps and retain context. FFprobe's documented timestamp metadata supports the timestamp-preserving approach.

Internal performance goals are p95 under 240 seconds and a watchdog with headroom before 300 seconds. These are engineering targets, not measured results. Queue time counts. Five 100-second jobs on one serial worker need about 500 seconds for the last job; a larger queue does not solve throughput. Clarify jury concurrency and test the actual envelope.

Meaningful test cases

Exercise missing/wrong key; multipart field correctness; two binary verdict invariants; 1–3 decodable JPEGs per event; multi-event ordering; synthetic VFR/rotated video with known timestamps; original-to-preview time alignment; spaces/Cyrillic filenames; malformed upload; restart during processing; OOM/timeout behavior; duplicate upload; renamed-file invariance; five queued requests; actual longest clip; and offline startup with weights already present. Tests must call the same processing path as production. A mock-only green build is insufficient.

Measure classification accuracy only after local type ground truth exists. The workbook's one time per positive row does not define full event intervals, all events or categories. Report the conflicting duplicate times separately until resolved. Always attach denominators to results. Twelve validation clips, even with twelve correct predictions, cannot prove universal accuracy.
